# 📊 Business Understanding

## Project Title

**Should Marketing Professionals Learn Data Analytics?**

*A Machine Learning Analysis of Education, Technical Skills, and Career Success*

---

# Background

The marketing profession has evolved significantly over the last decade. Organizations increasingly rely on data-driven decision-making, creating a growing demand for professionals who combine marketing expertise with analytical and technical skills.

As a result, many marketing professionals consider investing in Data Analytics education through university programs, certifications, or intensive bootcamps.

This project investigates whether education, professional experience, and technical skills are associated with career success using Machine Learning.

---

# Business Problem

Should marketing professionals invest in learning Data Analytics to improve their career success?

---

# Business Objectives

* Identify the factors associated with career success.
* Predict professional outcomes using Machine Learning.
* Evaluate the relative importance of education, experience, and technical skills.
* Provide data-driven recommendations for professionals considering a career transition.

---

# Research Questions

* Does education level influence career success?
* Which technical skills have the strongest impact?
* How important is professional experience?
* Can Machine Learning accurately predict career success?

---

# Hypotheses

### H1

Technical skills positively influence career success.

### H2

Higher education levels are associated with better professional outcomes.

### H3

Years of professional experience increase career success.

### H4

Technical skills contribute more to prediction accuracy than education alone.

---

# Stakeholders

* Marketing professionals
* Students
* Career changers
* Employers
* Recruiters
* Educational institutions

---

# Expected Outcome

Develop a Machine Learning model capable of identifying the factors that most strongly contribute to career success and providing actionable insights for professionals seeking to enhance their careers.
