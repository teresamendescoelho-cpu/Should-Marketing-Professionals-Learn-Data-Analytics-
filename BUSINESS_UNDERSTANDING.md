# 📊 Business Understanding

## Project Title

**Should Marketing Professionals Learn Data Analytics?**

*A Machine Learning Analysis of Education, Technical Skills, and Career Success*

---

# 📖 Background

The marketing profession has evolved significantly over the last decade. Organizations increasingly rely on data-driven decision-making, creating a growing demand for professionals who combine marketing expertise with analytical and technical skills.

As marketing becomes increasingly data-driven, professionals may benefit from developing skills in areas such as SQL, Python, Statistics, Data Visualization and Machine Learning.

This project explores the relationship between education, professional experience, career development indicators and Starting Salary.

The motivation for this project comes from my own career transition from Marketing to Data Analytics through the Ironhack Data Analytics Bootcamp.

Rather than relying only on personal opinion, this project uses data analysis, statistical methods, Machine Learning and visualization to investigate the factors associated with career outcomes.

---

# 🎯 Business Problem

### Should Marketing professionals invest in learning Data Analytics as a complementary skill to improve their career opportunities?

The project investigates whether education, practical experience, professional development and career progression are associated with **Starting Salary**, which is used as the main measurable indicator of career success in this analysis.

The project does **not** attempt to prove that learning Data Analytics directly causes higher salaries.

Instead, it aims to identify relationships and predictive patterns that can provide evidence-based insights for professionals considering the development of analytical skills.

---

# 🎯 Business Objectives

The main objectives of the project are to:

* Identify the factors most strongly associated with Starting Salary.
* Analyse the relationship between education, experience and career-development indicators.
* Evaluate the role of internships, projects and certifications in salary outcomes.
* Analyse the relationship between career progression and Starting Salary.
* Use SQL for data validation, exploration and aggregation.
* Use statistical analysis to investigate relationships between variables.
* Build and compare Machine Learning regression models to predict Starting Salary.
* Identify the most important predictive features.
* Translate analytical results into practical business and career insights.
* Evaluate whether Data Analytics can be considered a valuable complementary skill for Marketing professionals.

# ⚠️ Project Scope & Limitations

This project has several important limitations:

* The final Machine Learning dataset contains only **44 observations**.
* The SQL validation layer contains **400 records**.
* Starting Salary is used as a proxy for career success.
* The dataset does not directly measure whether a professional has learned Data Analytics.
* The analysis identifies associations and predictive patterns rather than causal relationships.
* Results should not be generalized to the entire Marketing profession.
* The strong performance of tree-based models should be interpreted cautiously because of the small modelling sample.
* A larger and more diverse dataset would provide stronger evidence.

---

# 🎯 Expected Business Outcome

The expected outcome is a data-driven understanding of the factors associated with career and salary outcomes.

The project aims to provide evidence that can help answer the broader question:

> **Can Marketing professionals strengthen their career profile by combining Marketing expertise with Data Analytics skills?**

The final conclusion should be interpreted as a recommendation about the **value of complementary analytical skills**, rather than proof that Data Analytics education directly causes higher salaries.
